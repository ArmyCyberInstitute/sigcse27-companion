# Multi-victim investigation: student questions

These exercises use a separately supplied assessment environment and case brief. The public demonstration does not include the assessment evidence, scenario generators, or answer-checking scripts.

## Investigation tasks

1. Identify the payments from the supplied victim addresses and calculate the total reported loss.

2. Identify the initial receiving addresses for the payments.

3. Trace the first layer of subsequent transfers and record the relevant outpoints.

4. Determine which later addresses receive funds from those transfers.

5. Identify any consolidation of the traced value.

6. Locate the candidate endpoint and justify it from the available history.

7. Calculate the value remaining at the endpoint and account for differences from the initial payments.

8. Explain how the evidence supports or limits a potential recovery calculation.

9. Prepare a report citing the transactions supporting your conclusions.
