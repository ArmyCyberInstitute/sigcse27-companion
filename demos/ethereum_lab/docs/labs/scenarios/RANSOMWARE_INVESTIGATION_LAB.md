# Ransomware investigation: student questions

These exercises use a separately supplied assessment environment and case brief. The public demonstration does not include the assessment evidence, scenario generators, or answer-checking scripts.

## Investigation tasks

1. Identify the payment from the supplied victim address. Record the amount and receiving address.

2. Identify the recipients of the next outgoing transactions and explain the evidence for including them in the trace.

3. Locate any convergence of the candidate paths.

4. Identify the endpoint at which the traced funds stop moving in the supplied evidence.

5. Explain what transaction history and balance observations support the endpoint conclusion.

6. Prepare an evidence-based investigation report, including uncertainty and alternative explanations.
